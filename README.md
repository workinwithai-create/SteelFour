# SteelFour

Four-bar live steel-string acoustic desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno and loop DAWs dump a beat and a vocal. The verse has no guitar. ChickFour mutes an electric. NeedThree plants nylon with eggs and a pad. Session players plant four bars of live steel-string — boom-chick, travis pick, open strum, hammer figure — so the vocal has a bed. SteelFour seats live FluidR3 chairs on that cut, plays dry vs steel, and exports a punch list, WAV, and MIDI.

Live samples only — FluidR3 acoustic guitar steel plus PreEight kit, piano, and bass. No oscillators. Audio never leaves the tab.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Amber Walk, Porch Climb, Fold Radio, Night Pew, Glass Lobby, Carbon Verse).
2. Hear **A · Dry** — kit, bass, quiet piano. It loops. Nothing rings.
3. Stamp a steel recipe.
4. Hear **B · Steel** — four bars with the live guitar. Then it stops. The next section would start on 5.
5. **4 · Steel only** isolates the chairs. Mute L / M / H.
6. Copy the punch list. Bounce WAV or MIDI and drop it on the unfinished verse.

Space plays B. A is dry. Esc stops.
